About Curo

Healthcare information should be easier to understand.

Curo is an academic product concept focused on one problem: important hospital information is often spread across different places, making it difficult to discover and compare.

CURO PRINCIPLEClear information before a difficult decision.
SimpleAccessibleComparable
Design principles

Calm, useful and human.

○

Plain language

Interfaces should remain understandable even when a person is unfamiliar with medical terminology.

Aa

Language access

Language options are part of the experience rather than an afterthought.

≡

Information hierarchy

Cost, distance, services and verification are surfaced before secondary details.

Important distinction

Curo is an information tool.

Hospital information can change. Costs, availability, insurance eligibility and service details should be confirmed directly with the provider before a healthcare decision.
