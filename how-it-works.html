A four-step journey

Designed around the way people actually search.

Each step keeps the amount of information manageable while still giving users enough context to make an informed next call.

1

Tell us what you need

Search using a condition, treatment, department or everyday phrase.

2

Choose a place

Use a city or locality to narrow results to hospitals that are practical to reach.

3

Understand the options

Review facilities, estimated cost ranges, distance, emergency services and other useful details.

4

Compare and confirm

Compare up to three hospitals, then contact the hospital to confirm current information.

Built for clarity

Important details stay visible.

₹

Estimated costs

See an indicative range before making a call. Actual charges should always be confirmed with the hospital.

⌖

Location first

Distance and locality are shown alongside the hospital name so the result is easier to understand.

✓

Verification cues

Verified and illustrative information are clearly separated so users know what needs confirmation.
