Privacy notice

Your information should be handled carefully.

This academic prototype does not currently send your search, saved-hospital or appointment-request data to a live backend.

Searches and saved hospitals used in the prototype are handled in the browser. A future production version would need a clear privacy policy, secure storage, consent controls and appropriate healthcare-data safeguards.

Do not enter sensitive medical records, payment information or other confidential information into this prototype.
